MWTC is organized by [Mid-MO Technology Group]({{< ref "sponsors/Mid-MO_Tech_Group.md" >}}), a local non-profit, and run on sponsor support. Sponsoring MWTC puts your organization in front of the Mid-Missouri tech community and helps keep the event accessible to attendees. Any funds left over after covering conference expenses are returned to sponsors or donated to an appropriate non-profit.

Pick the tier that fits, or [reach out](mailto:sponsor@mwtc.io?subject=Sponsor%20Opportunity&body=Hello!%20%0A%0AI%20would%20like%20to%20discuss%20becoming%20a%20sponsor%20for%20the%20Mid-West%20Tech%20Conference.%20I%20am%20interested%20in%20the%20%5BPlatinum%2FGold%2FSilver%2FBronze%5D%20tier.%20%0A%0AI%20can%20be%20reached%20at%20%5Bphone%20or%20email%5D) if you'd like to put together something custom or make an in-kind contribution.
